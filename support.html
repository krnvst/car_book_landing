Common questions

Where are my records stored?

On your iPhone. Keep PDF or text exports of important history and check your device backup settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a record from its menu, or delete a car in its editor to remove its associated history. Delete exported files separately. Device backups are managed through Apple settings.

Why are reminders not appearing?

Check notification permissions for CarBook in iOS Settings, maintenance dates and intervals, and Focus settings. Mileage-based estimates depend on the mileage you record.

Why is my QR export shorter?

QR codes have limited capacity. CarBook may shorten history to fit. Use PDF or text for a readable copy of larger histories.

Can the developer recover my data?

The developer has no copy of your local database. Check available device backups and previously saved exports.
