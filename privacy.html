CARBOOK / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. At a glance

CarBook is a personal vehicle service book. The developer does not collect vehicle records through the app, operate an account system, or receive your records on a developer-operated server. This policy covers the current app and this website.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores information you enter: vehicle make, model, year, VIN, license plate, mileage, vehicle photos, maintenance and repair records, dates, notes, costs, fuel or charging entries, and maintenance preferences. Records are stored locally using Apple SwiftData; preferences use local device storage. This information is used to display your service book, calculate summaries and maintenance schedules, and prepare exports you request.

3. Permissions and notifications

Camera access is optional and used to scan vehicle-history QR codes. Scanning is processed on the device; CarBook does not send the camera feed to the developer. The system photo picker lets you choose an image to save with your car; the app does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. Notification permission is optional and used for local maintenance reminders. Revoke camera or notification access in iOS Settings; remove a selected photo by editing the car.

4. Sharing and third parties

The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. PDF, text and QR exports may include identifying vehicle information and service details. Sharing occurs only when you choose to export or show a QR code. Check the contents and choose recipients carefully. Once shared, copies are controlled by recipients and the services you choose; their privacy policies apply.

5. Retention, deletion and backups

Records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Delete an individual record in its menu, or delete a car in its editor to delete the car and its associated history. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain it. Previously exported files and copies held by recipients must be deleted separately. iOS device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Backup retention and restoration are controlled by Apple and your device settings. The developer cannot access or remotely erase your local records.

6. Support correspondence

If you email skorns20@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you send to respond to your request. Do not send VINs, license plates or other sensitive records unless needed. Correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You may ask for access, correction or deletion by emailing the same address, subject to applicable legal requirements. Email is processed by the email providers involved.

7. This website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies. It stores your selected language and color theme locally in your browser; these preferences are not sent to the developer. Images and styles are served with the website. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical request data, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site under its own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Children and changes

CarBook is a general-purpose vehicle log and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s information. Changes to app functionality or data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the CarBook developer at skorns20@gmail.com.
